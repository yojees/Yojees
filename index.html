> WHAT I BELIEVE_

Core tenets shaping how I study, code, and approach technology.

01

DON'T WAIT TO BE READY

Competence isn't gained by waiting until you feel completely prepared. You become capable by diving in, confronting the unknown, and solving one obstacle at a time.

02

BUILD WHILE LEARNING

Passive consumption gives the illusion of mastery. Real understanding starts when theories and concepts are tested against actual code and live execution.

03

LEARN BY DOING

Debugging failed builds, resolving error logs, and breaking systems teach deeper, longer-lasting engineering intuition than memorizing textbook answers.

04

STAY CURIOUS

Never accept black boxes without asking how they tick: exploring how the operating system handles threads, how networks route packets, and how neural models infer.

05

BUILD WITH PURPOSE

Software is at its best when solving genuine, practical friction. Even small student projects should deliver real utility to someone in the world.
